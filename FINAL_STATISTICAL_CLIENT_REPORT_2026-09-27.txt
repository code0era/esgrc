## SPC-RPN Risk Assessment

### Data-Grounding Note (Required for Transparency)
The combined report provides **mean, UCL, LCL, Sigma_Level, RPN, and a "signals" count** per metric_id — it does **not** include raw time-series data, so specific control-chart rule violations (e.g., "Run of 8," "2-of-3 beyond 2σ") **cannot be confirmed or named** from this dataset; only the *aggregate signal count* is available as a proxy for instability. All 218 metrics report an identical **Sigma_Level of 3.0**, meaning control limits were uniformly set at ±3σ — this is a limit-setting convention, not itself evidence of stability or instability.

A structural pattern was confirmed in the source data: **RPN = signals × 35** holds exactly across every row (e.g., PBI10000: 59 × 35 = 2065; BCB10000: 10 × 35 = 350). This means the "signals" field is the sole differentiator driving RPN ranking — Severity and Detection sub-scores are **not separately reported** in this dataset. Per grounding rules, S/O/D values below are marked "Not decomposed in source" rather than invented.

### Process Stability Summary
Across the 218 modules assessed, all report a uniform 3.0 Sigma control-limit setting, with RPN values ranging from 350 (BCB10000) to 2065 (PBI10000) — a span driven entirely by differences in detected signal counts (10 to 59), since RPN = signals × 35 holds consistently across the dataset. Thirteen modules (RPN ≥ 1500) represent a concentrated high-risk cluster requiring immediate root-cause investigation, while a broad watch-tier band (~60 modules, RPN 1100–1470) warrants trend monitoring to prevent escalation. Because the source report lacks raw time-series data and explicit control-chart rule labels, all root causes above are stated as **hypotheses requiring further investigation**, and Severity/Detection RPN components could not be decomposed from the available data.
